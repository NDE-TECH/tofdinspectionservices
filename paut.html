PHASED ARRAY ULTRASONIC TESTING (PAUT)

PAUT inspection with planned beam coverage and recorded data.

Phased Array Ultrasonic Testing uses an array of elements with timed excitation to steer and focus ultrasonic beams. Its scan displays help trained personnel assess indications within an agreed examination volume.

HOW THE METHOD WORKS

Phased Array Ultrasonic Testing (PAUT)

A PAUT setup defines probe, wedge, focal laws, beam angles, scan positions and calibration. Sectorial scans can show multiple angles in a cross-sectional view, while encoded scanning can link the ultrasonic data to distance along a weld or component. The technique and scan plan must demonstrate the coverage needed for the specified inspection objective.

WHERE IT MAY HELP

Potential inspection applications

Weld examination

Examining accessible weld volumes in fabrication or service where material and geometry are suitable.

Component evaluation

Investigating defined areas of piping and other industrial components.

Complementary coverage

Pairing PAUT with TOFD when different sound paths and indication responses are useful under the approved procedure.

Applications are illustrative. Suitability depends on the component and approved inspection procedure.

TECHNICAL CONSIDERATIONS

Limits and complementary methods

Beam coverage and resolution vary with the probe, material, geometry and chosen focal laws. Coarse-grained or highly attenuating material, surface condition and restricted access may limit effectiveness. Data interpretation and acceptance remain tied to the approved procedure.
