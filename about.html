ABOUT THE COMPANIES

Inspection support from India and the UAE.

IAIS India and IAIS UAE support industrial inspection requirements through teams in Chennai and Abu Dhabi. This website presents their focused TOFD, PAUT, TFM and FMC inspection offering for clients with clearly defined technical objectives.

WHO WE ARE

Two companies. A focused conversation about your inspection scope.

Integrity & Advanced Inspection Solutions India Pvt. Ltd. is based in the Chennai region. Integrity & Advanced Inspection Solutions UAE LLC is based in Abu Dhabi. The companies' wider inspection services are described on their respective corporate websites.

For this specialist website, enquiries begin with the component, the inspection objective and the applicable requirements. These details help determine whether an advanced ultrasonic method is suitable and what should be included in the proposed scope.

IAIS TODAY

Industrial inspection with an advanced ultrasonic focus.

IAIS India’s wider published portfolio includes conventional and advanced nondestructive testing, in-service inspection, marine and offshore inspection, and related industrial technical services. IAIS UAE provides a regional point of contact for inspection and asset-integrity needs in Abu Dhabi and the wider UAE.

This site gives TOFD, PAUT, TFM and FMC their own clear technical context. The methods can be discussed individually or as part of a project-specific inspection plan, depending on geometry, material, access and the governing requirements.

PROJECT DISCUSSION

What helps us understand your requirement?

01

Asset and geometry

Component type, weld configuration, material, thickness and access conditions.

02

Inspection objective

Fabrication examination, in-service assessment, indication characterization or another defined purpose.

03

Acceptance and delivery

Applicable code or specification, reporting expectations, site location and target schedule.

The above is an enquiry checklist, not a statement that every technique is appropriate for every component.
