TOTAL FOCUSING METHOD (TFM)

TFM imaging for defined ultrasonic inspection objectives.

The Total Focusing Method reconstructs an ultrasonic image by focusing across a selected region of interest. It commonly uses Full Matrix Capture data and requires a suitable acquisition setup, material model and evaluation procedure.

Request a TFM proposal ↗

HOW THE METHOD WORKS

Total Focusing Method (TFM)

In a typical FMC/TFM workflow, an array acquires transmit-receive data from its elements. TFM processing applies calculated travel times to create an image for the chosen inspection region. The quality and meaning of that image depend on probe configuration, sound paths, material properties, calibration and validation.

WHERE IT MAY HELP

Potential inspection applications

Detailed indication evaluation

Reviewing the position and character of relevant indications where the method has been validated for the component.

Complex examination questions

Assessing whether focused images can provide additional information beyond a conventional PAUT presentation.

Procedure-supported comparison

Using TFM alongside other ultrasonic methods when the project procedure calls for complementary evidence.

TECHNICAL CONSIDERATIONS

Limits and complementary methods

A visually detailed image does not by itself prove detection performance or acceptance. Focusing quality can change with velocity assumptions, sound paths, attenuation and region size. Method selection and validation should precede field use.
